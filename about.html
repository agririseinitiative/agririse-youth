Who we are

About AgriRise

AgriRise Youth Initiative is a youth-led social enterprise initiative focused on sustainable agriculture, food availability and youth entrepreneurship. We aim to turn agricultural opportunity into practical skills, livelihoods and value for communities.

Our vision

A food-secure and empowered society

A food-secure and economically empowered society where young people lead sustainable agricultural innovation and every community can access nutritious, affordable food.

Our mission

Empowering people through agriculture

To empower young people through sustainable agricultural practices, appropriate farming technologies and entrepreneurship while improving food availability, creating employment opportunities and building resilient communities.

What guides us

Our objectives

Increase local production

Support the production of fresh vegetables that respond to local needs and market demand.

Build practical skills

Help young people develop agricultural, business and problem-solving skills.

Create opportunities

Develop pathways for youth participation, income generation and entrepreneurship.

Build a sustainable enterprise

Develop responsible operations, sales and partnerships that can support long-term work.

Our brand promise

We turn sustainable agriculture into an opportunity for young people and a source of fresh, affordable food for communities.
