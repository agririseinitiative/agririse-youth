Marketing & Brand Strategy

Our marketing will build trust, attract customers, connect with partners and develop a dependable market for the vegetables we produce.

Our brand identity

Tagline

A message of action today for stronger food systems in the future.

The marketing mix: 7Ps

Product

Fresh vegetables selected according to customer demand.

Price

Set prices using full production and delivery costs, local market prices and customer feedback.

Place

Direct orders, local markets, retailers and restaurant supply arrangements.

Promotion

WhatsApp Business, social media, samples, referrals and farm demonstrations.

People

Train team members in customer care, safe handling and communication.

Process

Clear order, harvest, quality-check, delivery and complaint-handling steps.

Physical evidence

Clean packaging, labels, signage and authentic project photographs.

First 90 days

Days 1–30

Research and prepare

Interview potential customers, compare prices, identify preferred crops and set up communication channels.

Days 31–60

Test the market

Share project updates, offer samples, approach shops and restaurants, and collect feedback.

Days 61–90

Improve and grow

Focus on products and channels showing demand; review margins, repeat orders and delivery reliability.
