Flagship initiative

AgriRise Hydroponic Farming Project

A proposed pilot exploring how vegetables can be grown without soil using nutrient-enriched water, while giving young people practical experience in agriculture and business.

01

Establish a pilot

Assess the site, water quality, energy access, system design and supplier costs before setting up a manageable growing unit.

02

Train young people

Provide practical learning in crop care, hygiene, harvesting, record keeping and basic agribusiness.

03

Reach local buyers

Test demand with households, retailers, restaurants and other potential customers before expanding production.

Potential crops

Leafy vegetables such as lettuce and spinach, subject to local demand, growing conditions and advice from a qualified agricultural specialist.

Potential customers

Households, food vendors, restaurants, retail shops, market traders and institutions where reliable supply arrangements are feasible.
